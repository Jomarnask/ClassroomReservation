Terms of Classroom Governance

PUP - Canlalay Campus Classroom and Facility Reservation System

Draft for review: These proposed terms are intended for the PUP-CRS academic project. They must be reviewed and approved by the appropriate campus authorities before official implementation.

1. Purpose and Scope

The Classroom and Facility Reservation System (CRS) is designed to help authorized students, faculty members, and administrators view classroom availability and manage classroom or instructional facility reservations. These terms establish proposed rules for responsible system use and reservation management.

2. User Accounts and Responsibilities

  • Users must provide accurate, complete, and up-to-date registration information.
  • Users must register using the institutional email address and other credentials required by the system.
  • Users must keep their passwords confidential and must not share accounts or allow unauthorized access.
  • Users must not impersonate another person, provide misleading information, or attempt to access accounts without permission.
  • Suspected account compromise, unauthorized activity, or inaccurate account information should be reported to the designated system administrator.

3. Classroom and Facility Reservations

  • Users must provide accurate information when submitting a reservation request, including the intended purpose, date, time, and facility where applicable.
  • A reservation request must not be treated as approved unless the system displays an approved status or the authorized administrator confirms the reservation.
  • Users must check the reservation status and any instructions issued by the authorized administrator.
  • Users must not knowingly submit duplicate, misleading, or conflicting requests to obstruct other users.
  • Facility availability shown in the system is subject to authorized scheduling changes and administrative decisions.

4. Use of Reserved Facilities

  • Users must use reserved facilities only for their approved academic or institutional purpose.
  • Users must observe applicable campus rules, safety procedures, and instructions from authorized personnel.
  • Users must take reasonable care of classroom furniture, equipment, and other facility resources.
  • Users must report damage, safety concerns, or facility problems to the appropriate campus personnel.
  • Users must leave facilities in an orderly condition after use and must not exceed their approved reservation period without authorization.

5. Cancellation and Schedule Changes

Users should promptly notify the appropriate administrator when a reservation is no longer needed or when a scheduling conflict arises. Changes to approved reservations must follow the procedures established by the campus. Authorized administrators may update schedules when necessary, subject to applicable campus policies and appropriate notification.

Specific cancellation deadlines, penalties, and rescheduling procedures must be established and approved by the campus before this section is enforced.

6. Prohibited Activities

Users must not:

  • Access or modify information without authorization.
  • Manipulate reservation records or attempt to bypass established approval procedures.
  • Disrupt system operation, interfere with other users, or intentionally submit false reservation information.
  • Use the CRS for unlawful purposes or activities that violate applicable university policies.
  • Copy, disclose, or misuse personal information obtained through the system.

7. Administrative Authority

Authorized administrators may manage user accounts, maintain classroom schedules, review reservation requests, and correct scheduling records within their assigned permissions. Such actions should be performed for legitimate institutional purposes and in accordance with approved campus policies.

Administrative access must not be used to make unauthorized changes, improperly disclose personal information, or bypass established procedures. Any restrictions, account suspensions, or other disciplinary consequences must follow applicable university rules and approved procedures.

8. System Availability and Limitations

The CRS is a scheduling and reservation management tool. System availability information may be affected by technical problems, maintenance, data entry errors, or authorized schedule changes. Users should follow official campus instructions when the system is unavailable or when reservation information appears incorrect.

The system does not replace official campus policies, safety requirements, or decisions made by authorized university personnel.

9. Data Privacy and Personal Information

The CRS may collect and process information necessary for account management, user identification, reservation scheduling, and system security. Such information must be handled in accordance with applicable privacy requirements, including the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), its implementing rules, and applicable university policies.

Personal information should be accessed only by authorized persons for legitimate purposes and protected against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss.

Before deployment, the responsible institution should provide an appropriate Privacy Notice explaining the personal data collected, purposes and legal basis for processing, recipients, retention period, data-subject rights, and the appropriate contact for privacy concerns.

Acceptance of these terms does not, by itself, replace any separate notice, consent, or other lawful basis required for particular data-processing activities.

10. Violations and Reporting

Users should report suspected misuse, unauthorized access, incorrect reservation records, or other system-related concerns to the designated administrator. Reported incidents should be reviewed through appropriate procedures. Any corrective action must be consistent with applicable campus rules and relevant requirements.

11. Amendments to These Terms

These terms may be revised when system functionality, campus procedures, or applicable requirements change. Proposed revisions should be reviewed and approved by the appropriate authority before being implemented. Users should be informed of material changes through an appropriate communication method.

12. Acknowledgment

By checking the acknowledgment box during registration, users confirm that they have had an opportunity to read these proposed terms and agree to comply with the applicable, officially approved rules governing their use of the CRS.

The system's terms and privacy notice should be presented accurately and should not imply institutional approval until that approval has been obtained.
