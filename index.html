How to Reserve a Room or Facility

Follow these simple steps to request classroom allocations, laboratory spaces, or audio-visual rooms at Polytechnic University of the Philippines - Canlalay Campus.

01

Log In or Register

Sign in to the CRS portal using your registered credentials. New students and faculty must create an account here.

Go to Login Page
02

Browse Available Rooms

Look for available classrooms, labs, or facilities by searching through floor levels.

Available in Dashboard
03

Submit Request Details

Select your preferred date, start/end time slot, course subject, and tag your professor.

Instant Slot Validation
04

Receive Confirmation

Track status updates (Pending, Approved, Rejected). Once approved, present your digital reservation slip to the campus guard or admin.

Digital Approval Pass

Reservation Eligibility & Rules by Role

Students

May submit classroom requests for official class make-ups, study groups, or recognized academic organization events.

Faculty & Instructors

Enjoy priority booking privileges for regular lectures, exam schedules, departmental meetings, and research labs.

Administrators

Review incoming requests, manage students registrations, and issue system-wide schedule adjustments.

About PUP Canlalay CRS

Empowering PUPians Through Digital Space Management

The Polytechnic University of the Philippines – Canlalay Campus Classroom & Facility Reservation System (CRS) was developed to organize space utilization and optimize academic scheduling across campus facilities.

By transitioning from manual pen-and-paper room logs to a transparent, real-time web portal, CRS empowers faculty and students to maximize instructional hours, prevent room conflicts, and foster a organized learning environment.

Transparent Allocation

Clear visibility into approved and pending room requests.

State University Standards

Built in strict compliance with PUP institutional policies and security guidelines.
